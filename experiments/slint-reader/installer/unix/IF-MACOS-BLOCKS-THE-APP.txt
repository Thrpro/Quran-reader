Quran Mushaf Reader is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID, so macOS
blocks it the first time you open it. To allow it, once:

1. Drag Quran Mushaf Reader to Applications and open it from there.
   Choose Done (not Move to Trash).
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security.
3. Beside the message about Quran Mushaf Reader, choose Open Anyway, and
   confirm with your password or Touch ID.

On macOS 14 and earlier, Control-click the app and choose Open instead.

Only do this for a copy from https://github.com/Thrpro/Quran-reader/releases.
If it still does not open, please report the exact message, your macOS version
and your Mac's chip (Apple menu > About This Mac):
https://github.com/Thrpro/Quran-reader/issues
