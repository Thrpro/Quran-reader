# Quran Mushaf Reader — review build 0.5.11

This local review follows 0.5.10. Public v0.5.0 corresponds to internal v0.5.4; the earlier patch notes retain that history. Existing downloads and source backups are preserved.

- Add a saved **Player position** setting: **Bottom (auto-hide)** or **Side**. Changing placement preserves the recording, position, speed and playing/paused state. The side player starts pinned, uses an existing gutter when there is enough room, and reserves space when necessary. A narrow window uses a compact side rail. Both placements leave the Quran and study panel unobstructed.
- Make the expanded bottom player about 60px tall on desktop, down from 100px. The measured reserved space falls from 120px to 76px. At 390px window width the player is 108px tall rather than 139px. Controls retain accessible labels, keyboard focus, pin/collapse and protected auto-hide behavior.
- In Arabic, progress starts on the right, elapsed time appears on the right, and duration appears on the left. Timestamps retain their readable digit order. Both seek sliders use one-second keyboard increments; the previous tenth-second increment could be lost when the playback clock rounded its displayed position to a quarter second.
- Reuse existing text bitmaps during the temporary reader-layout transform, then repaint at the final full density. First paints, changed artwork/theme and explicit fold snapshots still paint immediately. Canceled/replaced animations cannot strand a pending repaint. Keep hidden neighbors at the full reading-area density when the bottom player changes size, avoiding repeated allocations for all four previews; folds use those original pixels at equal or higher density. Automatic follow-along and player-space changes do not prepare unused 3D folds. A manual command still creates the same animated fold from the four neighboring previews, including while listening or paused.
- Validate saved whole-surah recordings through one cancellable stream consumer while building the playback Blob. Avoid the extra cloned response. Known-size ayah clips fill a single bounded final buffer and retain complete-MP3 checks. Late cache/download/append callbacks cannot revive a disposed or finished clip player. Audio bytes are unchanged; no transcoding or removal of recitation pauses is involved.
- Add Arabic/English placement, timeline direction, persistence, study-panel clearance and playback-continuity regressions. Extend rendering checks to hold and cancel a real layout animation and verify that existing canvases are not painted at intermediate sizes. Keep listening-memory measurements separate from concurrent builds and browser checks.

See MEMORY-PERFORMANCE.md for isolated listening measurements and their limits, and BUILD-VERIFICATION.md for validation scope. A smaller live canvas or fewer copies does not by itself establish lower total WebView2 RAM. Quran text, fonts, outlines, source page data and recitation quality are unchanged. No framework or dependency is added.

The pre-change source and portable app are backed up under outputs/backups/player-choice-from-0.5.10-2026-10-21. This work does not commit, push or publish a release.
