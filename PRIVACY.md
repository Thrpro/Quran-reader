# Privacy

Quran Mushaf Reader does not collect, store or send any personal information.
It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your computer

Your settings, bookmarks, reading position, and any recitations or tafsir books
you download are saved only on your computer, in the `SlintProfile` folder next
to the app. Nothing in that folder is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the folder
removes all of it.

## When the app connects to the internet

The Mushaf itself works fully offline. The app connects to the internet only
when you choose to listen to a recitation or download a tafsir book, and only
to fetch what you asked for:

| What you choose | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Verse-by-verse recitations | everyayah.com, verses.quran.foundation |
| Surah recitations and their verse timings | mp3quran.net |
| Tafsir books | api.quran.com |

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the file, such as the
reciter, surah or book. As with any download, those services can see your IP
address and what was requested; their own privacy policies apply to that.
